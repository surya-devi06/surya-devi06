      <p>
        <strong>GNN-Based Molecule Property Predictor & Explainability Platform</strong><br />
        <em>SRM TENSOR '26 Hackathon | Team Turbo | Problem Statement PS86</em>
      </p>
      <p>
        <strong>Problem Solved:</strong> Early-stage pharmaceutical drug discovery requires screening vast chemical spaces for ADMET (Absorption, Distribution, Metabolism, Excretion, and Toxicity) profiles before expensive lab synthesis.       </p>

          <li><strong>Explainable AI (XAI) Heatmaps:</strong> Visualizes atom-level attribution scores to show researchers the exact chemical substructures influencing property predictions.</li>
          <li><strong>Interactive Dashboard:</strong> Developed with <code>Next.js</code> and <code>Tailwind CSS</code> for high-throughput candidate sorting, Lipinski Rule-of-Five evaluation, and exportable reports.</li>
